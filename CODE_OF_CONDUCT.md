# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith, disagree about the work rather than the person, and leave the
project more welcoming than you found it.

## What is expected

- Treat everyone with respect, whatever their experience or background.
- Keep criticism about the code, the design, or the decision, and make it specific enough to
  act on.
- Accept that a maintainer's time is finite and that "not now" is a legitimate answer.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments, in the repository or anywhere the project is
  discussed.
- Personal or political attacks, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit consent.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, contact the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are handled
privately, and the person reporting is not expected to have resolved it first. A maintainer who
does not follow this code can be reported the same way.

## Scope

This applies wherever the project is represented: the repository, its issues and pull requests,
and any space where someone is acting on the project's behalf.
